          Frequently asked questions

          Where should I start?

          Everyone starts with Level 1, then Level 2. After that, choose the track that matches your role: Technician (Levels 3A and 4A) or Programmer (Levels 3B and 4B). Level 5 is for people who will train others.

          Do I need a technical background?

          Not for Level 1, which is open to everyone and assumes none. Later levels are written for specific roles, such as maintenance technicians and automation programmers.

          Which MoRo models does this cover?

          One curriculum covers the A-250, A-500 and A-1200. Where the models differ in ways that matter, such as navigation accuracy, the modules say so.

          How is the training delivered?

          Theory is online, at your own pace. From Level 2 upward, learning is combined with hands-on practice on a MoRo unit.

          How do I pass a module?

          Each module ends with a knowledge check. Score 60% or more to complete it. You can retake it as many times as you need.

          Do I get a certificate?

          You can download a certificate of completion for every module you pass. Level certification, which combines all the modules in a level with a level assessment, is in development.

          Is my progress saved?

          Yes, on the device and browser you are using. Signing in to carry your progress across devices is in development.
